OPM.js examples

Four-operator FM synthesis: from your first note to voice modulation, live audio routing, and offline WAV export.

Open live-audio examples over HTTPS or localhost, then click a button to start audio. Keep your volume comfortable; offline WAV rendering needs no AudioContext.

01 · Basic notes and voice banks

Choose a preset, pitch, and velocity. Play a short or held note, then release it manually.

OPM · parseVoiceBank · playNote · stop

02 · Melody and chord scheduling

Use Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star to explore melody, chords, stereo pan, and scheduling or cancelling future notes.

absolute at · lookahead · duration · stereo pan

03 · Live expression and LFO modulation

Bend and glide a held note, adjust expression and pan, and compare amplitude/pitch modulation without retriggering its envelope.

updateNote · pitch · glide · expression · modulation

04 · Shared AudioContext and routing

Borrow the host context, adjust GainNode volume, disconnect/reconnect routing, and verify that disposing the synth leaves host audio open.

context · destination · connect · close

05 · Offline PCM and WAV export

Render stereo PCM without starting live audio, inspect frame counts and diagnostics, then download PCM16 WAV.

renderNote · encodeWav · Float32Array

06 · Advanced controls and DX7 import

Explore key scaling, suspend/resume, diagnostics, and approximate six-to-four-operator DX7 SysEx conversion.

keyScale · diagnostics · importDX7

07 · Preset and DX7 audition

Compare presets and clean-room DX7 conversions across registers and velocities, inspect levels, and download rendered WAV.

preset quality · conversion limits · encodeWav

08 · Shared scores and interruption recovery

Pause, seek and loop a beat score, try sustain/mono priority, smooth timbre controls, DSP quality and atomic voice banks, or export multi-format WAV through a bounded Worker sink.

Transport · performance · PCM16/24/Float32 · Worker backpressure · recovery

09 · Adaptive game music

Switch exploration and combat on beat or bar boundaries, toggle layers while a shared pad keeps sounding, ramp tempo and swing patterns.

createArrangement · quantized sections · linear tempo ramps · voice priority

10 · Playable instrument and Web MIDI

Play a protected mono lead and a limited poly pad from keyboard, pointer or an optional user-granted MIDI input, with live per-part controls.

Performance · per-key controls · voice limits · optional Web MIDI

11 · Independent buses and stems

Run three OPM instances on one AudioContext with separate gain, filter/echo routing and offline stems.

shared AudioContext · per-bus saturation · stems

12 · FM sound designer

Edit four operators, algorithms, envelopes and selective LFO targets with a live signal graph, then export canonical patch JSON.

algorithm graph · dB envelopes · live controls · patch JSON

13 · Original songs and portable projects

Play two original FM songs, save and load canonical score projects, and download an offline WAV.

ScoreProject · beat compilation · Transport · WAV export
