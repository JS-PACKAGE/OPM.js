Playable instrument and optional Web MIDI

Two Performance parts: part 0 is a mono legato lead with voice priority 100; part 1 is a polyphonic pad limited to eight voices. Per-part controls update every sounding note and future notes. Begin with a low device volume.

Web MIDI is optional, requires a secure context and an explicit click, and is unavailable in some browsers. The adapter handles channel-voice notes, sustain, pitch bend, wheel, volume/expression, pan, aftertouch and all-notes-off; it is not a MIDI driver and ignores SysEx. Touch pressure, where supported, drives per-key modulation.
