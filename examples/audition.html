Preset and conversion audition

Compare all seven unchanged bundled presets and original, clean-room DX7 conversion recipes. Start with low device volume. Playback and downloaded WAVs use fixed host gain 0.12 (−18.4 dB); there is no automatic loudness matching or patch rewriting.

A/B sources and single-note settings

Every note has a 0.8-second gate followed by its release. The grid plays A then B at each register/velocity; release tails are separated. Stop cancels queued notes and releases active gates. Dispose disconnects and closes the owned AudioContext; Play can start a new session.

Download last rendered stereo WAV

WAVs are synthesized locally at 48 kHz with the selected register/velocity, including release tails. No recordings or patches are uploaded. Changing a setting invalidates the download.

Sound report

Choose sources, then measure their low/middle/high register and soft/medium/hard velocity grid.

Report values are raw DSP sample peak, gate RMS, and full-render RMS in dBFS, before playback gain. Unweighted RMS is an objective energy/loudness proxy, not LUFS or a perceptual quality verdict. Suggested trims are host-side attenuation only; more simultaneous notes need additional headroom.

Conversion limits

The DX7 examples are original generated parameter recipes, not third-party patch banks. OPM.js is four-operator synthesis, not a DX7 emulator. A/B compares converted recipes and bundled OPM voices; no original six-operator audio reference is rendered. Carrier removal, approximate envelopes/levels/velocity response, fixed-frequency key tracking, feedback placement and LFO differences can be audible.
