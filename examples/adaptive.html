Adaptive game music

Switch between exploration and combat on a musical boundary and add or remove layers while the pad keeps sounding. Begin with a low device volume.

No audio created.

Layers are aligned to the global beat grid, so a layer shared by two sections is one continuous schedule. A change is committed no earlier than the notes already admitted to the audio clock. Pause releases owned notes; Resume restarts musically from that beat and is not an exact DSP-state restore. Tempo ramps are linear in BPM across beats.
