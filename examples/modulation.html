Stereo and per-voice LFO

Play middle C with a copy of the brass voice to compare stereo pan, amplitude modulation (AM), and pitch modulation (PM). Headphones make left and right easier to distinguish.

Pan and modulation for new notes

Settings apply to new notes, not notes already playing. LFO belongs to the voice copy; there is no global setLFO. Replay releases the previous note, and stopping leaves its natural release tail.
