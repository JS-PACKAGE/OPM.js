Envelope in dB / seconds

  
  
  
ADSR times are seconds, sustain is linear amplitude; straight segments in dB imply exponential amplitude. −96 dB is the engine floor; start/release end become exact zero. Preview is a fresh note, not an oscilloscope: live ADSR edits re-anchor from current dB; key-rate scaling, pitch envelopes, velocity and LFO may alter the actual note.
